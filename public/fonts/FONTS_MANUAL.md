# Motion-video font manual

This library contains 74 font families and 103 `.otf`/`.ttf` files. Each family remains in its original folder so variants and license notes stay together.

## Important licensing rule

The included `info.txt` files identify many fonts as `Freeware, Non-Commercial`, `Demo`, or personal-use fonts. Do not assume a font is cleared for commercial Instagram, client, or monetized work. Check the linked license before publishing. The safest entries in this collection are the folders marked `Public Domain`, `SIL Open Font License (OFL)`, or `Freeware`—subject to their full license text.

## AI selection rules

- Use decorative/script fonts for short titles, names, quotes, and emotional overlays—not long paragraphs.
- Use bold/display fonts for hooks, numbers, thumbnail-style captions, and high-impact words.
- Use tech/condensed fonts for HUDs, data, sports, gaming, and futuristic motion graphics.
- Use outline, shadow, extrude, gradient, or 3D variants only for large display text where the effect remains readable.
- Keep body copy in a simple, highly legible font; this collection is primarily display-oriented.
- Tags below are practical style suggestions inferred from the family name and file variants; preview the font before committing to a design.

## Practical pairing guide

- Cinematic music: `kingsman`, `queensides`, `fasthin`, `home-video`, or `supercharge` depending on whether the tone is luxury, clean, nostalgic, or futuristic.
- Motivational music: `role-model`, `cement`, `blackrush`, `plaza`, or `sporting-outline` for short, high-impact words.
- Lifestyle/aesthetic music: `air-travelers`, `feelfree`, `golden-beach`, `palm-leaf`, `sellena-brush`, or `queensides`.
- Emotional/romantic music: `angelina`, `geraldine`, `glowbloom`, `mangolia`, `shalma`, `weddingday`, or `saltwater-type-italic-personal`.
- Comedy/food/pop music: `delicious`, `foodcan`, `lemondrops`, `icedsoda`, `mouldy-cheese`, or `super-funky`.
- Tech/gaming/sports: `supercharge`, `radeil-3d`, `moonkids-ext-bd`, `scoreboard-type`, `sporting-outline`, or `moogalator`.

The source URLs and license declarations are preserved in each family’s `info.txt`. Always verify the license for the specific publishing context before using a font commercially.
